About Herd UK

Herd UK is a daily polling app that asks Britain one question every morning. Users vote yes or no, then see how the nation responded — broken down by age, region, gender, and more.

Unlike social media polls, every Herd voter completes a one-time demographic profile during sign-up. That means our data tells a richer story than a simple headcount.
